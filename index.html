About Mary Ward Children's Home

Our background, mission, vision and values.

The story and purpose behind Mary Ward Children's Home.

Background

Established in 1993.

Mary Ward Children’s Home is registered a Welfare Organization that provides a friendly and family environment to the needy children, placed by the Department of Social Development. Mary Ward Children's Home was established by the Congregation of Jesus ( Mary Ward Sisters ) in 1993 due to the rise in the numbers of orphans and vulnerable children. It has a capacity of 60 children.

Mission

Our Mission

We provide a friendly and family environment to the needy children.

Vision

Our Vision

To transform the lives of our children into autonomous, invigorating, creative transparent, responsible and accountable citizens of Zimbabwe through empowering them to live their preferred ethical stories
